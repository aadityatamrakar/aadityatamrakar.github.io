01 · About

Techie, by profile and by habit.

I started publishing code in 2016 while freelancing: PHP and Laravel backends, Node.js APIs, and Ionic hybrid apps for food delivery, dealerships and small accounting shops. Each one shipped to a real customer.

Around 2019 the work turned toward markets. The option chain analyser for NSE Nifty became my most used project, followed by spread charts, broker SDKs and a fantasy cricket optimiser that is really a constraint solver in disguise.

A master's in computer science at UMass Boston added the systems depth: UI design research, C programming support for CS240, and a trading interface built for beginners. After two and a half years as a software engineer at Viasat, I went independent in 2026 to build trading tools, broker SDKs and write about what I learn.
